<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The data collected by the A2O are freely available to researchers, citizen scientists and the general public. These data support environmental science, including fauna surveys and ecological assessments, cross-disciplinary research among ecologists, biologists and computer scientists, citizen science projects, call-recogniser development, soundscape analysis and the exploration of acoustic methods.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Find data</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can <a href="/data/find-data/">find data</a> by site, ecoregion or participant. You can also <a href="https://data.acousticobservatory.org/regions">browse sites</a> and <a href="https://data.acousticobservatory.org/audio_recordings" data-type="URL" data-id="https://data.acousticobservatory.org/audio_recordings">audio recordings</a> without signing in. To participate in the analysis, log in or register for an account on the <a href="https://data.acousticobservatory.org/regions">A2O data portal</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Search for data</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can search through the A2O data using our <a href="https://search.acousticobservatory.org/" data-type="link" data-id="https://search.acousticobservatory.org/">search tool</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you have an audio snippet you want to search for, <a href="https://search.acousticobservatory.org/" data-type="link" data-id="https://search.acousticobservatory.org/">upload it to the search tool</a>, and we’ll find sounds that are similar.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Tip: you can download interesting segments to search for from our <a href="https://data.acousticobservatory.org/library" data-type="link" data-id="https://data.acousticobservatory.org/library">library</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
