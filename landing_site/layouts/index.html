The world’s first national acoustic observatory

The Australian Acoustic Observatory is a nationwide sensor network capturing the sounds of wildlife and ecosystems across seven major ecoregions. Its openly available recordings support ecological research, conservation and a deeper understanding of environmental change.

Explore Australia’s Acoustic Data

Access acoustic recordings collected by hundreds of sensors across seven Australian ecoregions. The A2O dataset also includes valuable site information, such as vegetation cover, landform, environmental disturbance and photopoints, providing richer context for ecological research and conservation.

Browse data by site, ecoregion or participant without signing in. To use the analysis tools and contribute to research, log in or create an account on the A2O data portal. All data are available under a CC BY 4.0 licence.
